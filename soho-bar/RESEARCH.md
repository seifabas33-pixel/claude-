## 6. Destination Context — Port Ghalib (for the website's "Find Us" / SEO copy)

- Port Ghalib is a Red Sea marina resort town **~3–4 km (5 minutes) from Marsa Alam International Airport (RMF)**.
- The international marina accommodates up to **1,000 yachts** and is an official Port of Entry to Egypt.
- The waterfront promenade ("the Corniche") hosts **140+ venues** for dining, entertainment and shopping, including the traditional bazaar **The Khan**.
- The area is world-famous for diving/snorkeling (Brothers Islands, Daedalus Reef, Rocky Island, house reefs) — the guest base is largely European holidaymakers (English, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish), which argues for a **multilingual website**.
